About the prices.

Prices change occasionally — coffee from the roaster, butter from the dairy, the cost of keeping the lights on through a King’s Lynn winter. We don’t print them on the website because they would always be slightly out of date.

The chalkboard above the counter has the current numbers. They are reasonable, the portions are honest, and a pot of tea will always be a pot of tea, not a single cup pretending to be one.
