About the café

A small place, quietly run.

Silver Teaspoon is an independent café on Norfolk Street, in the old part of King’s Lynn — a stretch of red brick, sash windows, and pavement that catches morning light.

The idea behind Silver Teaspoon was modest from the start. Somewhere to sit down properly with a pot of tea. Somewhere a coffee was made with thought, not speed. Somewhere a sandwich was put together while you waited and a slice of cake was cut to the size you actually wanted.

Norfolk Street felt like the right address for that. It’s a working street — pawn shops and antiques, the bookseller, the bakers, a barber, a tailor — the kind of high street that still has its own weather. People walk through. People stop. People come back.

The café sits at number 126, between the old shopfronts. The room is small. The chairs aren’t all matching. The window looks out at the rain when there’s rain and at the market crowds on a Tuesday and Saturday.

We serve loose-leaf tea brewed in pots, espresso and filter from a small roast list, hot chocolate when the temperature drops, and a counter of cakes and bakes that changes through the week. There are sandwiches and toasties made to order, and a soup that depends entirely on the season.

None of this is grand. None of it is trying to be. The teaspoon is silver because every café used to have one, and because the small things — what stirs the tea, what cuts the cake, what carries the milk — are the ones that say whether a place was thinking about you or not.

That’s the long and short of it. Come and find us when you’re passing.

“Tea by the pot. Cake by the slice. A corner you can sit in for an hour.”

Where to find us.

126 Norfolk Street, King’s Lynn, Norfolk · PE30 1AP. Walking distance from the bus station, Tuesday Market Place, and the river.

No bookings — just walk in. A table is usually being cleared.
