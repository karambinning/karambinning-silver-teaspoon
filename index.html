A teaspoon’s worth of unhurried.

The Silver Teaspoon sits on Norfolk Street — a few doors along from the bakers and the bookshop, opposite where the morning light catches the brickwork.

The idea is small and simple. Loose-leaf tea brewed properly. Coffee that has had thought put into it. Cake, sandwiches, and the kind of corner-table you can sit at for an hour without anyone minding.

If you’ve been wandering up from the Tuesday Market or stepping in from the rain — kettle’s on. Come and find us.

What’s on today.

The board changes through the week. A short list of things we make, served from open until just before close.

What follows is the shape of the menu — for the day’s specifics, the chalkboard above the counter is the source of truth.

To drink

Loose-leaf tea (a small board of blends), espresso drinks, filter coffee, hot chocolate, soft drinks.

To eat

Bakes and cakes from the counter, sandwiches and toasties made to order, soup when the weather asks for it.

To take away

Anything from the counter, packed for the walk. Reusable cups always welcome.

A quiet corner of Norfolk Street.

You’ll find us between the antique frontages of old King’s Lynn — a short walk from the river, the bus station, and the Saturday market.

Pavement’s wide enough for a pram, the door’s usually propped open in the warmer months, and the chairs at the back catch the afternoon sun for about an hour each day. It’s a good hour.
